# Exploratory research

The central question is whether apparent allowance reductions remain visible after accounting for the workload, API price changes and telemetry defects. An executive report should answer that question directly. The dossier should supply the denominators, reproducible calculations and limits needed to assess the answer.

The current [inventory](../source_inventory.json) covers the rollout union, broker and guardian history, recovery logs, historical PM records, prior usage and cost reports, provider pricing and six exact subscription mailboxes. The retained evidence already supports controlled bank and model comparisons. It also exposes substantial cloned/replayed history and incomplete billing and service-tier information.

The strongest negative result is the similarity between early directly recorded banked windows and nearby ordinary-compatible windows at common API prices. Later Astra and Sol cohorts differ, but date, effort, banking, task mix and unobserved service tier still limit causal interpretation. The report should preserve both results.

Remaining research directions are independent reconciliation with the prior turn ledger, interpretation of historical lease conflicts, remaining reset narratives, private historical numeric reports and accessible billing evidence for two non-M365 identities. These are evidence questions that the existing mandate authorizes the agent to pursue.

No client proposal, contract, new budget or operational change is being proposed. Unavailable absolute hidden credits and unverifiable invoices should be reported as measurement limits, not filled with assumptions disguised as observations.
