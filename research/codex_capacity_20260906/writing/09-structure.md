# Report structure and section goals

| Section | Reader purpose and required facts | Component |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Executive report | Answer value, trend, account differences, banking and model/effort questions in roughly two pages. Include the EUR200 benchmark, corrected bank comparison, Astra association, sample sizes and actionable measurement gaps. | Historical cost ledger: executive summary |
| Scope and source inventory | Explain population, cutoff, all accessible source categories, dates, counts, missing evidence and prior-ledger reconciliation. | Usage-history forensics: source evidence table |
| Accounting and cohort methods | Explain cumulative/copy/replay correction, retry treatment, identity, leases, concurrent workloads, reset epochs, model/effort dominance and rounding/timing sensitivities. | Usage-history forensics: cross-source proof and evidence classes |
| Pricing and account value | Give dated actual-model and constant-price definitions, FX/tax/service-tier assumptions, per-account/month benchmark multiples and exposure gaps. | Historical cost ledger: price scenarios and period totals |
| Capacity over time and models | Compare controlled hours and epochs, sample counts, token components, cache shares, model/effort and period overlap. State which rankings are unidentifiable. | Historical cost ledger: integrity sensitivities and operational patterns |
| Banked resets and Reddit | Give direct/inferred/ambiguous reset evidence, revised ordinary controls, same-account comparisons and a fair recent-period allegation assessment. | Usage-history forensics: cross-source proof; timeout diagnosis: distinction before mechanism |
| Findings and measurements | At least ten numbered evidence-backed findings, confidence, alternative mechanisms and exact observations needed. | Disk-capacity incident: findings, causal alternatives and repeatable verification |
| Reproduction and artifact map | Give runnable small-table regeneration and authorized raw-source pipeline, frozen inputs, versions, hashes and validation limits. | Historical cost ledger: reproducibility and artifacts |

Charts will show account-month reference value, dated versus fixed-price hourly associations, and bank/ordinary/ambiguous epoch comparisons. Tables will carry observation counts and denominator qualifications. Methods, citations and reproducibility are reader-required content for this dossier. Writing-process narration stays here.
