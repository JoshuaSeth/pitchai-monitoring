design directions. Every direction is grounded in the same production
inventory, safe-evidence boundary, retained-state sources, 60-domain scale, and
five-tab information architecture. It is not permission to substitute local
artwork for a missing Pro target.

## Selection

Concepts 01, 03, and 04 are the three selected deterministic builds. Concept 01
now exercises a critical production database-auth incident as its active
inspection. All three share fixtures and reusable Jinja2/Tailwind components.

## Retention and comparison gate

Exactly ten images are complete in one authenticated visible ChatGPT Pro
conversation. The final image completed at 2026-08-26 17:32:41 UTC; all ten
visible Save-button downloads were retained after 17:52:42 UTC. Selected 01,
03, and 04 targets are compared with their deterministic renders. Passing
reports require full-frame perceptual similarity above 96.0% plus visual review
of each unnormalized amplified diff; self-comparison, crops, and ignored-region
masks are forbidden.

The completed scores are 96.4097% for concept 01, 97.6084% for concept 03,
and 97.0438% for concept 04. File hashes, image-specific analysis, raw score
diagnostics, and selected-build implications are recorded in
