## Selection

Concepts 01, 03, and 04 are the three selected deterministic builds. Concept 01
now exercises a critical production database-auth incident as its active
inspection. All three share fixtures and reusable Jinja2/Tailwind components.

## Open design gate

The final-concept submission timestamp and its required 20-minute wait have not
started because the visible Pro session is not authenticated. The comparison
directory must remain empty until the real Pro targets are retained. Passing
reports require a composite above 96.0% and a visual review of each amplified
diff; self-comparison and ignored-region masks are forbidden.
